# Policy

## Source treatment

- The operator's beliefs enter through prose processed in the same way as other
  sources.
- Curatorial actions include selecting sources to process and verifying or
  editing synthesis, definitions, and statements.

## Manual reproducibility

- The procedure must be documented sufficiently for the operator to perform it
  manually. The operator should repeat the manual process periodically.
- Automation is limited to operations the operator understands and can replicate.

## Documentation

- Define terms in [definitions.md](definitions.md) before making them part of the
  method.
- Procedures link to the relevant entries in [definitions.md](definitions.md)
  rather than repeating their definitions.
- Procedural requirements state required operations and completion checks.
- Put counterexamples in a separate section when they are used.
- Keep the library README factual and concise.
- Keep the handoff to a brief focus for the next spike. Put orientation material
  in the README or formal documentation.
- Preserve discussion, corrections, and approvals in the [source threads](threads/README.md).
